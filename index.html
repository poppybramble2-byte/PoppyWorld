The world

A valley where everything is round

The Little Bramble Gang is a family of round, pebble-shaped animal friends who live in a soft valley of hills, ponds and tiny gardens. Every pal is built from circles and rolled edges, so a small hand can fill each shape in one stroke.

Line rules for all 15 pictures: one bold outline with rounded caps and joins · every fillable area is a closed white shape with a thin dashed grey edge · 6 to 14 regions per page, each wider than a crayon tip · eyes and noses are the only solid black marks · no text, no letters, no logos.

Colour guide
To trace and colour

Guide tip:

Your crayons

Tap a shape to brush its dashed edge and fill it with the chosen crayon. The guide is a suggestion, never a rule.

Two activities on every picture

Brush the dashes, then colour it in

Every picture is printed twice: a small colour guide, then the same drawing as dashed line art on white. Brushing first builds the hand; colouring second rewards it.

01

As printed

Clean white shapes with thin dashed grey outlines. The dashes show exactly which areas are yours, and where the edge is.

02

Brush the outline

Trace each dashed line once, slowly, with a brush pen. Thick where you press, thin where you lift.

03

Colour inside

Fill each shape you traced. One crayon stroke should cover any shape in the book.

For the brush stage

  • Any brush pen, felt tip or thin marker
  • Start at the top of a dashed shape and go all the way round
  • Big shapes first, little ones last
  • Wobbly outlines are the point: nothing to erase

For the colour stage

  • Each guide names every colour it used
  • White shapes take light crayons and pencils cleanly
  • Eyes and noses are already black, so leave them
  • One picture can be done twice: brush it today, colour it tomorrow
